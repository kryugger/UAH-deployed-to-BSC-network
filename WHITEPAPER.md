UAH Token — Whitepaper
1. Introduction

UAH Token is a community-driven utility and governance token designed to enable decentralized coordination, transparent decision-making, and sustainable on-chain participation. The project focuses on real DAO mechanics rather than speculative hype.

2. Vision

The vision of UAH Token is to create a modular ecosystem where governance, treasury management, and community incentives operate fully on-chain and evolve through collective decisions.

3. Core Utility

UAH Token is used for:

DAO governance and voting

Proposal creation and execution

Access to reward pools and participation mechanics

Gamified community engagement (badges, tiers, incentives)

4. Governance Model

UAH Token holders participate in governance through a DAO system. Voting power is determined by token balance and participation logic. All proposals and treasury actions are transparent and executed on-chain.

5. Treasury

The treasury is controlled exclusively by DAO governance. Funds are allocated through approved proposals for ecosystem development, rewards, and future integrations.

6. Token Philosophy

UAH Token follows a utility-first approach. The token is not designed as a speculative instrument but as a coordination tool for decentralized infrastructure and community-driven initiatives.

7. Roadmap (High-Level)

DAO and treasury deployment

Governance activation

Community reward mechanics

Dashboards and ecosystem tools

Progressive decentralization

8. Disclaimer

UAH Token does not represent equity, shares, or financial instruments. Participation is voluntary and subject to smart contract logic and community governance decisions.
